Brian Davis is a seasoned technology leader, passionate innovator, and engaging speaker on a mission to make AI practical and useful from day one. His sessions are known for live demos, no-fluff insights, and practical examples you can take straight back to your own codebase. Whether you're building apps, automating workflows, or exploring what’s next, Brian brings clarity, creativity, and a contagious excitement for what AI can unlock.
