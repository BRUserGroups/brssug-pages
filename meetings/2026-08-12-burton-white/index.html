How AI is Changing Successful Engagements in the Current Federal Landscape

Burton White

Wednesday, August 12, 2026 · 5:15 PM

Burton White, CEO of Excella, will discuss how AI is changing the roles, skills, and team structures needed to deliver successful engagements in the current federal landscape. All are welcome. Bring a friend!

Burton White, Chief Executive Officer and Co-founder at Excella, is a trailblazing leader with over 30 years of experience in fostering community impact and driving technological innovation. Under his command, Excella has grown from its inception to a brand recognized for delivering innovation solutions to clients across the commercial and public sectors. With over three decades of experience in the technology consulting space, Burton is hyper focused on developing and living a strategy that drives value for clients, Excellians, and the community at large. His strong belief in empowering people with technology positions Excella as a leader in cutting edge technologies such as AI. As a New Orleans native, Burton is deeply committed to creating an inclusive and equitable tech community and champions a "connect, share, and learn" philosophy that enriches the local tech culture. His passion for service extends outside of technology, and manifested in Excella's community impact program, ImpaX, a companywide initiative that contributes to multi-year projects that make a difference.
